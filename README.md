Summary of KZN Municipality Airports

-A qualitative case study was done to investigate provincial government intervention in developing struggling provincial airport authorities (Richards Bay, Margate and Pietermaritzburg) as regional economic development catalysts.
-Interpretive approach and cross-sectional research methodology were used, qualitative data collection through semi-structured questionnaires were conducted from airport managers to determine important challenges facing them.
-Important issues identified include poor fire and rescue services, poor regulatory compliance, insufficient revenue generating capacity, and inadequate capacity to retain aviation skills.
-Recommended actions for KZN government include structured grants, integration of economic development planning and building of capacity. This project has improved my strategic management, analysis skills and leadership skills.

Research Overview

Regional airports play a crucial role in economic connectivity

This research examines the challenges facing municipal-owned airports in KwaZulu-Natal and proposes strategic interventions to enhance their economic contribution to regional development.

Research Context

Airports play a vital role in global transportation networks, facilitating the movement of people, goods, and services, while also serving as catalysts for economic growth. In the context of KwaZulu-Natal (KZN), a province with a diverse economic landscape in South Africa, municipal-owned airports emerge as fundamental assets capable of driving regional growth and development.

Current Challenges

Infrastructure Deficits

Many airports suffer from outdated or insufficient infrastructure limiting their growth potential.

Funding Constraints

Limited investment hampers ability to upgrade facilities and improve service quality.

Operational Inefficiencies

Inefficient processes lead to increased turnaround times and higher operational costs.

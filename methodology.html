Research Design & Methodology

Comprehensive research approach for assessing airport support mechanisms

Research Design

  • Data: Primary Qualitative
  • Reasoning: Inductive
  • Timeline: Cross-sectional
  • Design/Strategy: Case Study

Population & Sample

  • Population: 9 Municipal-Owned Airports
  • Sample: 4 (Purposive)

Data Collection & Analysis

  • Data Collection: Open Questionnaires
  • Data Analysis: Thematic Analysis

Research Framework

Ontology/Epistemology: Relativist/Interpretivist

Research Approach: Qualitative, exploratory study providing a snapshot of current state

Methodology: Case study approach with surveys administered to airport managers

Participating Airports

The research focuses on municipal-owned airports in KwaZulu-Natal, including:

  • Mkuze Airport
  • Richards Bay Airport
  • Margate Airport
  • Ulundi Airport
  • Dundee Airport
  • Virginia Durban Airport
  • Newcastle Airport
  • Pietermaritzburg Airport
  • Ladysmith Airport

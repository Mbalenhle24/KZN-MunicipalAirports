Core Problem

KZN municipal-owned regional airports are not operating optimally and not contributing meaningfully to economic development.

Primary Research Question

What actionable recommendations can be proposed to enhance the strategic support for KZN municipal-owned airports to maximize their role in regional economic development?

Key Challenges

Inadequate Infrastructure

Many airports suffer from outdated or insufficient infrastructure, limiting capacity to handle modern aircraft.

Limited Funding

Lack of investment for maintenance, expansion, and operations hampers ability to upgrade facilities.

Operational Inefficiencies

Inefficient processes lead to increased turnaround times and higher operational costs.

Lack of Aviation Expertise

Absence of qualified personnel results in fragmented efforts and missed opportunities.
